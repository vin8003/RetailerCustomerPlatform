# Promotional email strategy

- **Status:** Deferred (not current implementation)
- **Ticket:** [KAN-16](https://vin8003.atlassian.net/browse/KAN-16) · [snapshot](../tickets/KAN-16.md)

Dispatch promotional email to registered store customers. Proposed campaigns:

| Campaign | Trigger | Audience |
|----------|---------|----------|
| Welcome 10 | Signup complete | New retail customers — 10% code |
| Recover Cart | Cart idle > 4 hours | Users with items in cart |
| We Miss You | No order > 30 days | Inactive historical buyers |

Also contemplated: holiday / segment bulk offers.

Do not treat this file as shipped behaviour until implementation exists in code.
