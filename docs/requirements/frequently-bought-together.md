# Frequently bought together (cross-sell)

- **Status:** Proposed (not assumed shipped)
- **Ticket:** [KAN-17](https://vin8003.atlassian.net/browse/KAN-17) · [snapshot](../tickets/KAN-17.md)

Show high-affinity add-ons from the current cart on product detail and cart, to raise AOV.

- Example: cart contains milk → suggest bread, eggs, cereal.
- Placement: below cart lines on mobile; sidebar on desktop.
- UI: horizontal product cards with add-to-cart.
- Metric: recommendation clicks and checkout conversion.

Do not invent extra recommendation products or ML scope beyond this.
