# Purchase bill image (attach-only)

- **Ticket:** [KAN-60](https://vin8003.atlassian.net/browse/KAN-60) · [snapshot](../tickets/KAN-60.md)
- **App:** retailer inward purchase new/edit

A purchase can attach **one** supplier-bill image (photo or file) and show it later on that bill.

**Out of scope:** OCR / auto-fill from the photo. Do not build that on this requirement.
