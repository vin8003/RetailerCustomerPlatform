# OE-143 F-0052 — Khata credit limit and lock (thin slice)

Work snapshot. Durable: [khata-credit-lock.md](../requirements/khata-credit-lock.md).

Problem: credit limit existed on `RetailerCustomerMapping` and POS blocked over-limit after creating then deleting the order. Due days and audited override were missing. F-0064 bill-wise is not this ticket.

Done in this change (EXTEND, one slice):

- `credit_due_days` (null = no due-days lock) + `outstanding_since` on the mapping (running outstanding clock)
- POS credit finalize evaluates limit + overdue **before** insert; 400 unless `credit_override`
- Override reuses existing `orders.update` (catalog has no finance / credit-override code); writes `OrgAuditLog` `credit_override`
- Payment / cash POS remain allowed while locked
- Unauthorized mutate of limit/due days stays **403**; cross-tenant mapping mutate stays **404** deny

Out of scope: bill-wise allocation (OE-129), FE lock badges, SupplierLedger, second Order table, live `*.ordereasy.win`.
