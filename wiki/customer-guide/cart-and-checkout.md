# Cart and checkout

Your cart holds everything you want to order from **one shop**. Checkout is where you confirm delivery, payment, and place the order.

## Your cart

Open **Cart** from the bottom bar to review:

- Each product name, quantity, and line price
- Subtotal before offers and delivery
- Applied discounts or reward points (if any)

You can:

- Increase or decrease quantity
- Remove items
- Continue shopping (go back to catalog)

**One shop per cart:** If you want to order from two different shops, you need two separate orders.

## Checkout steps

### 1. Delivery or pickup

| Mode | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| **Delivery** | Shop brings the order to your address (if they deliver to your area) |
| **Pickup** | You collect from the shop when it is ready |

Only options your shop supports will appear.

### 2. Delivery address

For **delivery** orders:

- Select a saved address or **add a new one**
- Include landmark and phone number for easy finding
- Double-check pin code and flat/house number

For **pickup**, you may not need an address — the shop location is shown instead.

### 3. Payment method

Common options:

| Method | How it works |
|--------|--------------|
| **Cash on Delivery (COD)** | Pay the delivery person or at pickup in cash |
| **UPI** | Pay via UPI when you receive the order (shop’s UPI) |

The shop receives payment directly. OrderEasy records the payment type for the shop’s records.

### 4. Reward points

If you have **loyalty points** with this shop:

- You may apply them at checkout for a discount
- Points are **per shop** — not transferable between shops

→ [Rewards and referrals](rewards-and-referrals.md)

### 5. Special instructions

Optional field for notes like:

- "Ring the bell twice"
- "Substitute only if same brand"
- "Call before delivery"

Keep instructions short and practical.

### 6. Review and place order

Check the final total including:

- Item subtotal
- Delivery charge (if any)
- Discounts / points applied

Tap **Place order**. You should see a confirmation screen with your order number.

## After placing the order

- Order appears in **Orders** as **Pending**
- Shop will confirm or contact you if something is wrong
- You get notifications as status changes

→ [Track your order](track-your-order.md)

## Common checkout issues

| Issue | What to do |
|-------|------------|
| Minimum order not met | Add more items or choose pickup if allowed |
| Delivery not available | Shop may be outside your radius — try pickup |
| Cart empty after signup | Rare glitch — add items again; report to support if it persists |
| Payment option missing | Shop may only accept COD — check with shop |
