# Find your shop

OrderEasy shows you **local shops** you can order from. You pick one shop and shop only from their catalog.

## Allow location (recommended)

When you open the customer app, it asks for your location. Say **yes** if you are comfortable — this is the fastest way to see shops near you.

**What happens:** The app sorts shops by distance and shows delivery/pickup options for your area.

## Pick a city manually

If you decline location or GPS is unavailable:

1. You will be asked to **select your city**
2. The app shows shops registered in that area
3. You can still order — distance may be less precise

## The shop list

Each shop card typically shows:

| Info | What it tells you |
|------|-------------------|
| Shop name | Which kirana you are looking at |
| Distance | How far from you (when location is on) |
| Rating | Feedback from other customers |
| Delivery / Pickup badges | What this shop offers |
| Minimum order | If the shop set one for delivery |

Tap a shop to enter their storefront.

## Choosing the right shop

**OrderEasy is not a marketplace** — you are not comparing prices across fifty shops on one cart. You choose **one shop per order**.

Tips:

- Pick the shop you **already buy from** — that is who OrderEasy is designed for
- Check if they offer **delivery to your address** or **pickup only**
- Look at **operating hours** — some shops may not accept orders outside open hours

## Inside the shop

Once you select a shop, you see **only that shop’s products**:

- Categories (snacks, staples, household, etc.)
- Search within this catalog
- Product details with live price and availability

To order from a different shop, go back to the shop list and switch — your cart is per shop.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| No shops shown | Check city selection; shop may not be on OrderEasy yet in your area |
| Shop too far | They may not deliver to you — try pickup or another shop |
| Cannot find my usual kirana | Ask the shop owner if they are on OrderEasy; they need to register |
