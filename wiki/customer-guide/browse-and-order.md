# Browse and order

Once you have chosen a shop, you can explore their catalog and add items to your cart.

## Ways to browse

### Categories

Most shops organise products into **categories** — for example:

- Rice and dal
- Snacks and biscuits
- Beverages
- Household items

Tap a category to see all products in it.

### All products

A full catalog view shows everything the shop has listed online. Useful when you know the shop well and want to scroll.

### Search

Use the **search bar** to find products by name. Search works within the **current shop only**.

Tips for search:

- Try common names ("Tata salt", "Amul milk")
- If you do not find something, the shop may not have listed it online yet — you can ask via order chat after placing an order, or visit in person

## Product page

Tap a product to see:

| Detail | Why it matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Name and image | What you are buying |
| Price | Current selling price set by the shop |
| Unit / pack size | 500g, 1L, pack of 6, etc. |
| Availability | In stock or not |
| Description | Extra info if the shop added it |

## Add to cart

Tap **Add to cart** to add the item. You can:

- Change quantity in the cart later
- Keep browsing and add more items
- Open **Cart** anytime from the bottom navigation

## Wishlist

See something you buy often but not today? Tap **wishlist** (heart icon) to save it for later.

Wishlist is per shop — saved items stay tied to that retailer’s catalog.

## Guest browsing

You **do not need an account** to browse or add to cart. Sign up is usually required at **checkout**.

Your cart is saved as a **guest cart** until you log in. After signup, items should still be there.

## Out of stock items

If a product shows as unavailable:

- You cannot add it to cart
- The shop may restock soon — check back later
- For urgent needs, call or visit the shop directly

## Bottom navigation

When shopping, you will usually see:

| Tab | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| Home | Back to shop or shop list |
| Cart | Review items and go to checkout |
| Wishlist | Saved products |
| Profile | Account, orders, addresses |
