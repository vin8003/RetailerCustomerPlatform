# Getting started — customers

This guide is for **shoppers** who want to order from a local shop using OrderEasy.

## Step 1: Open the customer app

Go to **[customer.ordereasy.win](https://customer.ordereasy.win)** on your phone or computer.

No install is required — it works in your browser.

## Step 2: Share your location (or pick a city)

When you first open the app:

1. Allow location access if prompted — this helps show **shops near you**
2. If location is not available, you can **pick your city manually**

You will see a list of shops in your area.

## Step 3: Choose your shop

Tap the shop you want to order from. Remember: you are ordering from **that specific kirana**, not from "OrderEasy" as a seller.

Each shop shows useful info such as:

- Distance from you
- Whether they offer delivery, pickup, or both
- Ratings from other customers

## Step 4: Browse and add to cart

Inside the shop you can:

- Browse **categories** (dal, snacks, household, etc.)
- Search for products
- Open a product to see price, unit, and availability
- Tap **Add to cart**

You can add items **before creating an account** — your cart is saved as a guest cart.

## Step 5: Sign up or log in (when ready)

When you go to checkout, you may need to:

- **Sign up** with your phone number (OTP verification)
- Or **log in** if you already have an account

Your guest cart should carry over when you sign up — you should not lose items you already added.

## Step 6: Checkout

At checkout you will:

1. Choose **delivery** or **pickup** (only options your shop supports)
2. Add or select a **delivery address** (for delivery orders)
3. Choose **payment** — usually Cash on Delivery or UPI
4. Apply **reward points** if you have any
5. Add special instructions if needed
6. Place the order

## Step 7: Track your order

Go to **Orders** to see status updates:

- Pending — shop has received your order
- Confirmed — shop accepted it
- Processing / Packed — being prepared
- Out for delivery or Ready for pickup
- Delivered — complete

You may get **push notifications** when the status changes.

## Tips for a smooth experience

| Tip | Why |
|-----|-----|
| Order from one shop at a time | Each shop has its own cart |
| Save addresses in your profile | Faster checkout next time |
| Check delivery vs pickup | Not every shop delivers everywhere |
| Use wishlist for repeat items | Quick access to favourites |
| Message the shop via order chat | For questions about substitutions |
