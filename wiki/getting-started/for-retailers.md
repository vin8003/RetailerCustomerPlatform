# Getting started — retailers

This guide is for **shop owners and staff** setting up OrderEasy for the first time.

## Step 1: Open the retailer app

Go to **[retailer.ordereasy.win](https://retailer.ordereasy.win)** in your browser (phone or computer).

## Step 2: Create your shop account

1. Tap **Sign up**
2. Enter the **access code** provided to you (this links your account to OrderEasy onboarding)
3. Complete registration with your shop details
4. Log in with your new credentials

If you already have an account, go straight to **Log in**.

## Step 3: Set up your shop profile

Before taking orders, complete your shop basics under **Profile**:

| Setting | Why it matters |
|---------|----------------|
| Shop name and contact | Customers see this when they find you |
| Shop location (map pin) | Customers discover nearby shops |
| Operating hours | Customers know when you are open |
| Delivery / pickup options | What you offer online |
| Delivery radius and charges | If you deliver |
| Payment methods | COD, UPI, etc. |

→ Details in [Shop settings](../retailer-guide/shop-settings.md)

## Step 4: Add your products

You have three ways to build your catalog:

| Method | Best for |
|--------|----------|
| **Add one product at a time** | Small catalog or new items |
| **Excel bulk upload** | Large existing list |
| **Scanner app + Bulk Add** | Walking the shelf with a phone |

Start with your most-ordered items. You can always add more later.

→ [Products and stock](../retailer-guide/products-and-stock.md)

## Step 5: Try the POS counter

Open **POS Billing** and run a test sale:

1. Search or scan a product barcode
2. Add items to the bill
3. Optionally attach a customer
4. Take payment (cash, UPI, credit, or split)
5. Complete and print receipt

This confirms your catalog and prices work before going live online.

→ [POS billing](../retailer-guide/pos-billing.md)

## Step 6: Share your shop with customers

Tell your regular customers to open **[customer.ordereasy.win](https://customer.ordereasy.win)** and search for your shop name.

They do not need a special code — they find you by location and shop name.

## Step 7: Handle your first online order

When an order comes in:

1. Open **Orders** — you will see it as **Pending**
2. Review items and quantities
3. **Confirm** if you can fulfil, **modify** if something needs changing (customer must approve changes), or **Cancel** if you cannot
4. Move through: Processing → Packed → Out for delivery / Ready for pickup → Delivered

→ [Online orders](../retailer-guide/online-orders.md) · [Order statuses](../shared/order-statuses.md)

## Daily routine (suggested)

| Time | Task |
|------|------|
| Opening | Check pending online orders, confirm what you can do today |
| All day | Bill walk-ins at POS; advance online orders as they are ready |
| Stock-in | Record supplier purchases when goods arrive |
| Closing | Review sales on dashboard, check credit balances |

## Optional but valuable

- Set up **offers** for app or POS customers
- Add **suppliers** and track **supplier khata** (what you owe them)
- Enable **customer credit** for trusted regulars
- Install the **scanner app** for faster catalog building
