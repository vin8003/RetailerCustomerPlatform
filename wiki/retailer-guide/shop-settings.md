# Shop settings

Configure how your shop appears to customers and how online ordering behaves.

## Profile

**Profile** holds your shop’s public identity:

| Setting | Customers see this |
|---------|-------------------|
| Shop name | On shop list and storefront |
| Description | Short intro optional |
| Phone | For calls about orders |
| Logo / images | Branding in app |
| Payment methods accepted | At checkout |

Keep name consistent with what locals call your shop — easier to find.

## Shop location

Pin your shop on the **map**:

- Customers use this for distance and discovery
- Set pin at your actual storefront
- Update if you move (rare)

Customers may be asked for **their** location at app start — your pin is where **you** are.

## Operating hours

**Operating hours** define when you are open each day:

| Day | Open | Close |
|-----|------|-------|
| Monday–Saturday | e.g. 8:00 | e.g. 21:00 |
| Sunday | Optional different hours |

Customers may still place orders outside hours depending on settings — clarify your policy with staff.

## Delivery and pickup

Configure what you offer:

| Option | Settings |
|--------|----------|
| **Pickup** | Always available if enabled — customer collects |
| **Delivery** | Delivery radius, minimum order, delivery charge |

Be realistic with radius — overpromising causes bad reviews.

### Delivery charges

Pick a **fee type** in Profile → Delivery:

- **Flat fee**: one delivery charge per order.
- **By distance**: fee bands such as "up to 2 km ₹20, up to 5 km ₹35". Past the
  last band you can add a **per-km rate** (for example ₹8 for every started km
  beyond 5 km). Without a per-km rate the last band's fee applies up to your radius.
- **Free delivery above** a cart value works with either type.
- No delivery (pickup only)

Distance is measured in a straight line from your shop's map pin to the
customer's address pin. Set your **map pin** in Profile, or distance pricing and
radius cannot apply: customers then see your shop by city/pincode and pay the
flat delivery charge.

### Delivery radius and who sees your shop

Customers who share their location only see shops whose **delivery radius**
covers them, and checkout refuses delivery to an address outside it. Customers
who only picked a city still see shops in that city.

## Payment settings

Enable methods you actually accept:

- Cash on delivery
- UPI on delivery
- Credit for known customers (POS mainly)

Mismatch between settings and reality frustrates customers.

## Notifications

Ensure staff devices allow **push notifications** for new online orders. Missing alerts means delayed confirmations.

## Access and staff

Currently one retailer account model per shop onboarding. Train all staff on:

- Who confirms online orders
- Who can change prices
- Who handles supplier payments

Document internal roles even if the app has one login today.

## Before going live checklist

- [ ] Shop name, phone, and location correct
- [ ] Operating hours set
- [ ] Delivery/pickup rules match what you tell customers
- [ ] Top products added with prices
- [ ] Test order from customer app completed once
