# Offers and promotions

**Offers** let you run discounts and loyalty programmes for **POS customers**, **app customers**, or both.

## Types of offers

Common offer types on OrderEasy:

| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| **Percentage off** | 10% off snacks category |
| **Flat off** | ₹20 off orders above ₹500 |
| **Buy X Get Y (BXGY)** | Buy 2 soaps, get 1 free |
| **Cart value deal** | Free item above ₹1000 |
| **Loyalty points** | Earn 1 point per ₹10 spent |

Exact options depend on how your shop configures each offer.

## Creating an offer

1. **Offers** → **New offer**
2. Choose offer type
3. Set rules (products, categories, minimum cart, dates)
4. Choose channel: **POS**, **customer app**, or **both**
5. Set start and end dates (optional)
6. Save and activate

Test with a small cart before promoting widely.

## Where customers see offers

| Channel | Experience |
|---------|------------|
| **Customer app** | Discount at checkout if cart qualifies |
| **POS** | Staff sees eligible offers when billing |

## Loyalty points

Points programmes encourage repeat visits:

- Customer **earns** on completed orders
- Customer **redeems** at checkout
- Balances are **per shop**

Configure earn rate and redemption rules in the offer setup.

## Referral offers

Reward customers who bring friends:

- Referrer gets bonus points
- New customer may get welcome discount

Manage codes from offer or rewards settings.

## Best practices

| Do | Avoid |
|----|-------|
| Time-bound festival offers | Permanent deep discounts you cannot sustain |
| Clear rules ("min ₹300") | Confusing BXGY that staff cannot explain |
| Train staff on POS offers | Changing prices manually AND running duplicate offers |
| Review offer cost in reports | Stacking too many offers without margin check |

## Ending an offer

Deactivate or set end date — existing carts may still apply rules until checkout completes; new orders follow updated rules.
