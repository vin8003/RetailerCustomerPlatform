# What OrderEasy is not

These points come up often. Getting them clear upfront saves confusion for both shops and customers.

## Not a marketplace

OrderEasy is **not** Swiggy, Zepto, or Amazon for groceries.

- Customers **choose one specific shop** they already know
- There is no feed of random products from hundreds of stores
- Each shop has its own catalog, prices, and delivery rules

**What this means for customers:** You are ordering from *your* kirana, not from "OrderEasy" as a seller.

**What this means for retailers:** You are not competing with other shops on the same screen. Your customers see *your* store.

---

## Not a delivery company

OrderEasy does **not** provide riders or a delivery fleet.

- Delivery and pickup are **the shop’s choice**
- The shop sets delivery radius, charges, and minimum order if applicable
- When an order is "out for delivery", it means **your shop** (or your person) is delivering — not an OrderEasy driver

There is no separate rider app. Logistics stay with the retailer.

---

## Not a payment gateway (yet)

For most orders today:

- **Cash on delivery (COD)** and **UPI** are the main payment modes
- The shop receives payment directly from the customer
- Credit (khata) sales are tracked in the system but settled offline between shop and customer

OrderEasy records *how* payment was made; it does not replace your bank account or UPI QR in most flows.

---

## Not a replacement for your relationships

OrderEasy helps organise orders and stock. It does not replace:

- The trust between a shopkeeper and regular customers
- Local credit arrangements you already have
- Phone calls for urgent or unusual requests (though chat is available for order-related messages)

---

## Not only a customer app

The **retailer side** is equally important — POS, purchases, supplier khata, and inventory are core features, not add-ons.

A shop can use OrderEasy mainly for counter billing and gradually turn on online ordering when ready.

---

## Summary

| OrderEasy is… | OrderEasy is not… |
|---------------|-------------------|
| Software for one local shop | A multi-shop marketplace |
| Counter + online in one stock book | A separate delivery startup |
| Your shop’s catalog online | A generic product database |
| Khata and supplier tracking | A bank or payment processor |
