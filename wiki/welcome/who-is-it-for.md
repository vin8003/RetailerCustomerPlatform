# Who is OrderEasy for?

## Shop owners and staff (retailers)

OrderEasy is built for **kirana stores, provision shops, and local retailers** who want:

- A proper billing counter (POS) with barcode scan, credit sales, and receipts
- Online ordering from existing customers — without building a separate stock book
- Simple stock and purchase tracking
- Customer credit (udhaar / khata) and supplier payment tracking
- Offers and loyalty for regulars

You do **not** need a big team or IT department. If you can use a smartphone and a laptop, you can run OrderEasy.

**Typical retailer tasks:**

| Task | Where in OrderEasy |
|------|-------------------|
| Bill a walk-in customer | POS Billing |
| Accept an online order | Orders |
| Add a new product | Products |
| Record stock from a supplier | Purchases |
| Check what a customer owes | Customers → Credit |
| Run a discount or offer | Offers |
| Scan many products quickly | Scanner app + Bulk Add |

→ [Retailer getting started](../getting-started/for-retailers.md)

## Customers (shoppers)

OrderEasy is for people who **already know and trust a local shop** and want a easier way to order:

- Browse the shop’s real catalog (not a random marketplace)
- Add items to cart and checkout in one flow
- Choose delivery or pickup (whatever the shop offers)
- Track order status without calling repeatedly
- Earn reward points and use referral codes

You are **not** browsing hundreds of unknown shops. You pick **your** shop and order from them.

**Typical customer tasks:**

| Task | Where in OrderEasy |
|------|-------------------|
| Find nearby shops | Home → shop list |
| Order groceries | Shop → Cart → Checkout |
| See where my order is | Orders |
| Save favourite items | Wishlist |
| Use reward points | Checkout or Rewards |

→ [Customer getting started](../getting-started/for-customers.md)

---

## When OrderEasy is a good fit

✅ You run one shop (or one branch) and want counter + online in one system  
✅ Your customers are mostly local regulars  
✅ You handle your own delivery or pickup  
✅ You want khata/credit tracking for trusted customers  
✅ You want to reduce phone/WhatsApp order chaos  

## When to look elsewhere

❌ You want to list on a marketplace with thousands of competing shops  
❌ You need a dedicated delivery fleet and rider app  
❌ You run a chain with complex central warehousing (OrderEasy is built for single-shop workflows first)
