# What is OrderEasy?

OrderEasy is software that helps a **local shop** run its daily business and take orders from customers who already buy there.

Think of it as a digital helper for your kirana, provision store, or neighbourhood shop:

- **At the counter** — bill walk-in customers, take cash or UPI, track credit (khata), print receipts
- **On the shelf** — know what is in stock, what you bought from suppliers, and what is running low
- **On the phone** — let regular customers browse your real catalog and place orders without endless WhatsApp messages
- **Behind the scenes** — run offers, loyalty points, and keep supplier payments organised

## One shop, two sides

Every shop on OrderEasy has:

| Side | Who uses it | Where |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **Shop (retailer)** | Owner and staff | [retailer.ordereasy.win](https://retailer.ordereasy.win) |
| **Customer** | People who buy from that shop | [customer.ordereasy.win](https://customer.ordereasy.win) |

Both sides share the **same stock, prices, and orders**. When you sell something at the counter, online stock updates. When a customer orders online, you see it in your shop dashboard.

## A day in the life

**Morning:** You open the shop, check pending online orders, and confirm what you can fulfil today.

**During the day:** Walk-in customers are billed at the POS. Online orders move from confirmed → packed → out for delivery or ready for pickup.

**Evening:** You record any stock received from a supplier, check customer credit balances, and review the day’s sales.

**Anytime:** A regular customer opens the customer app, picks your shop, adds items to cart, and checks out — without calling you for every item.
